OctaWrite — Privacy Policy Last updated: 29 August 2026 OctaWrite is an IELTS Writing practice app for Android, published by Monu Sidhar. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your writing stays on your phone, there is no account, and the only things that use the internet are the AI evaluation you ask for and the ads in the free version. 1. Your essays stay on your device Your drafts, attempts, feedback, bookmarks, streak and settings are saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also delete everything from Settings → Reset progress. 2. AI evaluation When you ask for an AI score, a grammar check, an outline or a vocabulary upgrade, the text you submit is sent over an encrypted connection to our relay server and from there to our AI provider (Groq, Inc.) to generate the reply. The text is used only to produce that reply. It is not stored on our server, not used to train any model, and not linked to you. If you never use an AI feature, no writing leaves your device. 3. What we collect Nothing that identifies you. The app has no account, login, name, email or phone number, and no analytics. When the app is installed it generates a random identifier that is attached to AI requests solely so the free daily allowance can be rate-limited per device. It is not connected to you or your Google account and changes if you reinstall. 4. Ads The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and usage signals to show and measure ads, under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice in the app at any time. Premium removes all ads. 5. Payments Premium is purchased through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. The app keeps only a record that a purchase succeeded so it can unlock Premium, and Google's subscription service confirms it. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Play Store → Subscriptions. 6. Reminders If you turn on the daily practice reminder, it is scheduled on your device by Android itself. No notification is sent from a server. 7. Data retention We hold no personal data, so there is nothing to retain or delete on our side. Submitted essay text is discarded by the relay as soon as the AI reply is returned. 8. Children's privacy OctaWrite is intended for people preparing for the IELTS exam and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. 9. Third parties The only third parties that receive any data are Google (AdMob, Play Billing) and Groq (AI replies), each for the purpose described above. We do not sell data to anyone. 10. Changes If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and inside the app, with a new "last updated" date. 11. Not affiliated OctaWrite is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to IELTS, the British Council, IDP Education or Cambridge Assessment English. 12. Contact Questions about privacy?
