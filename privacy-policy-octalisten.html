OctaListen — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026
App: OctaListen — IELTS Listening (Android package com.monusidhar.ielts_listening_2026)
Developer: Monu Sidhar
Contact: monusidhar91@gmail.com

OctaListen is an IELTS Listening practice app. It works without an account and is designed so that your study data stays on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and why.

1. What stays on your phone

The following is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded:

  • Your test attempts, answers, scores and band estimates
  • Your progress history, streaks, listening minutes, target band and exam date
  • Dictation and trainer records, saved words and your "known words" list
  • Settings (theme, accent and voice preferences, playback speed, reminders)
  • Cached AI explanations you have already requested
  • A random install identifier (see section 3)

You can delete all of it at any time: Settings → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

2. Audio: the app plays, it never records

Every recording you hear is generated on your phone by its own text-to-speech engine from a written script. OctaListen does not request the microphone permission, does not record audio, and does not send audio anywhere. Your phone's text-to-speech engine (for example Google Text-to-Speech) is a separate app governed by its own policy; OctaListen only asks it to speak text on the device.

3. What leaves your phone, and when

FeatureData sentTo whomWhen
AI explanations ("Ask Octa") The question text, the accepted answer, your answer, a few lines of the transcript around the answer, and the random install identifier. Never your name, email or any other content. Our own proxy server (Cloudflare Workers), which forwards the request to the AI provider (Groq) and returns the explanation. The proxy applies rate limits per install identifier to prevent abuse. Only when you tap an AI feature. Cached explanations are served from your device without any request.
Ads (free version only) Advertising identifier and device information as described by Google's policies. Google AdMob and its mediation partners. When an ad is requested. Ads are shown on menu screens only — never while a recording plays, in review, in a drill or in a trainer round. Premium removes ads entirely.
Purchases Handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive a purchase token to confirm your subscription; we never see payment details. Google Play. When you buy, restore or manage a subscription.
App analytics and crash reports Anonymous usage events (for example "test submitted", "drill completed", "paywall viewed" — never answers or free text), device model, OS version, and crash stack traces. Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. While the app is in use.

4. The install identifier

On first launch the app generates a random identifier that is not linked to you, your Google account or your device's hardware. It is used only to apply fair-use limits on AI explanations and is reset when you clear the app's data or reinstall.

5. Consent for personalised ads

In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA and UK), a consent form is shown before any ad loads. You can change your choice in the app's privacy settings at any time. If ads cannot be shown without consent, the app simply shows no ads.

6. Notifications

The optional daily practice reminder and the one-time "your trial ends tomorrow" notice are scheduled locally on your device. No push service and no server is involved, and they can be switched off in Settings.

7. Children

OctaListen is intended for people preparing for the IELTS exam and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention

On-device data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. The AI proxy does not store request content; rate-limit counters expire within 24 hours. Analytics data is retained according to Google Firebase's standard retention settings.

9. Your rights

Because the app holds no account and no personal profile, the data you control is on your device. To delete it, use Settings → Clear all data or uninstall. For any question or request about this policy, email monusidhar91@gmail.com.
