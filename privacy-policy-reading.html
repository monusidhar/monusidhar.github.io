Privacy Policy

App: IELTS Reading 2026 (package com.monusidhar.ielts_reading_2026)
Last updated: 13 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the IELTS Reading 2026 mobile application (the “App”) handles your information. By using the App you agree to the practices described below. We designed the App to be privacy-first: your study data stays on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device only (using Android local storage) and does not upload it to any server we control:

  • Your practice history, scores and band results
  • Saved vocabulary words, bookmarks and reading progress
  • Your settings (target band, exam date, theme, reminders, font size)
  • In-progress test drafts and daily-challenge cache

You can erase all of this at any time from Settings → Clear all data.

2. AI features (text you choose to analyse)

When you use an AI feature — answer explanations, performance analysis, the daily reading challenge, or word lookup — the App sends only the relevant text needed to generate that response (for example, a passage excerpt, a question, your answer, or a single word and its sentence) to our AI provider, Groq (groq.com/privacy-policy). We do not send your name, email, contacts, location, or any device identifier with these requests. AI features are optional; the App is fully usable without them.

3. Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain device information and identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. Purchasing the one-time “Remove Ads” product removes all ads.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (the lifetime “Remove Ads” product) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment-card details. Your purchase status is stored locally so the App can unlock premium features.

5. Analytics

The App may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymous usage (such as which screens are opened) so we can improve the App. This data is not used to identify you personally.

6. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

7. Children

The App is intended for general audiences preparing for the IELTS exam and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Your choices

  • Disable ads by purchasing the Remove Ads product.
  • Reset your Advertising ID from your device’s Google settings.
  • Clear all stored data from within the App at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact
