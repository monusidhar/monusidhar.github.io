Privacy Policy

App: OctaSpeak: IELTS Speaking (package com.monusidhar.ielts_speaking_2026)
Developer: Monu Sidhar · Last updated: 22 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the OctaSpeak mobile application (the “App”) handles your information. By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. About this app

OctaSpeak is an IELTS Speaking preparation application. It provides cue cards, Band 7–8 sample answers, vocabulary lists, AI feedback on your spoken answers, and timed practice modes.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to IELTS, the British Council, IDP Education, or Cambridge Assessment English.

2. Information we collect

The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account. A random identifier is generated when the App is installed and is sent with AI requests so that abuse can be rate-limited; it is not linked to you or your device and changes if you reinstall the App.

Your bookmarks, practice history, settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. Only the data described under “AI feedback”, “Advertisements” and “Internet access” below ever leaves your device.

3. Local storage

The App stores the following on your device only:

  • Your bookmarked cue cards
  • Your dark-mode preference and other settings
  • Your practice history, scores and transcripts
  • Your premium / subscription status

This data can be cleared at any time by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it. Device backups of this data are disabled.

4. AI feedback (voice recordings and transcripts)

When you use AI practice features (AI Speaking Coach, Daily Question, Full Mock Interview), your voice recording is sent securely (HTTPS) to our server and forwarded to our AI provider, Groq, to transcribe it and generate your band score and feedback.

Recordings and transcripts are processed and then discarded — we do not keep them on any server. The feedback and transcript you see are saved only on your device as part of your practice history. Groq processes this data under its own privacy policy.

5. Advertisements

The free version of the App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob and its mediation partners (Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize). These networks may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalisation as described in their privacy policies. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later.

You can remove all ads by subscribing to Premium. Premium users are never shown advertisements.

6. In-app purchases

The App offers auto-renewing Premium subscriptions (weekly, monthly and 6-month plans; the monthly plan may include a free trial) through Google Play Billing. All payment processing, trials, renewals and cancellations are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not store or process any payment card information.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in the Google Play Store. Your purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored on any device using the same account.

7. Internet access

The core study features of the App work fully offline. Internet access is used only for:

  • AI feedback, transcription and question generation
  • Loading advertisements (free version only)
  • Verifying in-app purchases through Google Play
  • Anonymous crash reports and usage analytics (Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics)
  • Checking for app updates (a small version file)

Beyond the AI requests described above, no user data is sent to our servers.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at monusidhar91@gmail.com. We aim to respond to all enquiries within 7 business days.
